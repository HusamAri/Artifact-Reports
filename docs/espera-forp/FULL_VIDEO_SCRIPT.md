# ESPERA — Chapter VI: Federica · FULL VIDEO SCRIPT (events + dialogue)
Curated Chaos · Director Hüsam Arı · ~4:55 · 21:9 anamorphic · Languages: Spanish · Italian · Turkish
Reflects the locked screenplay v1 PLUS this session's director revisions (scream→black ending, "Un Segundo Después"
coda, gold/black/marble palette, and the added supernatural witch-battle power layer).

Language key — **ES** Spanish (Castilian, her chosen/free tongue) · **IT** Italian (her native tongue, only when she
loses control) · **TR** Turkish (Marmo's kill line + the Şile daily talk). Federica accent = Italian; Husam = Turkish/İstanbul.

## ACT I — ARRIVAL (0:00–0:45) · silent, ambient
**Events.** Rainy İstanbul night. Federica rides alone in the back of a taxi — fur-collar dark coat, blue lapis pendant
faint at her chest, no choker. Rain and broken neon smear the window (no legible text). She watches the street, calm as
glass; her lip parts once as if to speak, then stops. Her phone glows in her coat pocket — she rests a hand over it,
doesn't answer. She takes it out; the screen shows a single letter: **F**. She holds it in her lap, doesn't lift it to
her ear.
> **FEDERICA (ES, low, almost to herself):** *"Ya casi. Espérame."* — (Almost there. Wait for me.)

She pockets the phone. The taxi stops. She steps out onto wet cobblestones and walks to a heavy wooden door with her
signature carriage — chin up, one brow raised, fast sure steps — face never shown. The door closes behind her.

## ACT II — LA TAVOLA (0:45–1:35) · silent + two short lines
**Events.** A dark Italian corridor (faded portraits, deep-red carpet); she lowers her coat off one shoulder as she
walks. She enters a candlelit salon: black marble table, a banker's lamp, a carved lapis globe, a tall Venetian mirror
on the rear wall. She sits, clasps her hands under her chin — the consigliera's waiting pose. The lapis pendant warms
from within, an ember glow spilling warm gold onto her collarbone and jaw.

From a velvet curtain at the back, a tired silhouette appears — **OLD HUSAM**, backlit, face soft and unreadable.
> **OLD HUSAM (TR, low):** *"Federica…"*

She lifts her head; her face fills the frame (the one deliberate look to camera in the whole film).
> **FEDERICA (ES, firm, near a whisper):** *"Espera. Un segundo."* — (Wait. One second.)

They hold a still gaze; cigar smoke rises; the pendant's ember pulses once. No one moves.

## ACT III — VERDICT ARRIVES (1:35–2:00) · Destrucción cello drone · Cinestill 800T · BLACK palette
**Events.** Hard cut; the air has hardened. Federica now wears a thin, slightly bent gold laurel crown and a dark navy
sweater off the shoulders, right fist on the black marble. The pendant CORRUPTS — the blue turns to obsidian, the gold
cage blackens, fine BLACK cracks branch under the skin of her neck (the destrucción form of her power). Her fist opens;
both hands press flat to the marble; her gaze drifts to the rear mirror. Face close-up, lips moving:
> **FEDERICA (IT, to herself, whisper):** *"Tu non sai cosa hai fatto. Ma io lo so."* — (You don't know what you've
> done. But I do.)

## ACT IV — THE MIRROR (2:00–2:50) · Sprechgesang build
**Events.** Federica walks to the Venetian mirror. The reflection is not her — it is **MARMO**, her pale white-marble
double: same face carved in stone, a marble torque collar, a dark oxblood velvet imperial mantle, a thorny asymmetric
laurel, a corrupted pendant. They stand at the same level, staring. Over her shoulder, deep in the mirror world, OLD
HUSAM waits, tired, eyes down.

Marmo steps out of the glass into the salon and offers a marble palm; Federica hesitates, then takes it. A slow,
controlled, epic tango begins between the living woman and her stone double, the mirror always behind them.
> **FEDERICA (IT, whisper):** *"Chi sei tu davvero?"* — (Who are you, really?)
> **MARMO (IT, glass-cold):** *"Sono la parte di te che ti ha protetto. Adesso proteggo anche la sua."* — (I am the part
> of you that protected you. Now I protect his too.)

Federica turns her head to the mirror, to Old Husam.

### ⚡ POWER LAYER — THE WITCH DUEL (added supernatural sequence, escalates the Act IV climax)
**Events.** The tango breaks into a duel of powers, in the same salon, both in full costume (Federica: navy sweater +
gold laurel + Foglia d'Oro pendant; Marmo: oxblood velvet mantle + marble + thorny laurel). Palette law — **GOLD =
Federica's living power, MARBLE + gold kintsugi = Marmo.**
- **Gilded Veins / Awakening:** molten-gold veins bloom and pulse up Federica's neck and hand; the pendant blazes.
- **Gold Fall:** she opens her hand and a storm of razor-edged gold leaves erupts and sweeps outward like blades.
- **The Duel — Gold vs Stone:** Federica hurls the gold-leaf storm; Marmo raises a slab of cracked marble as a shield,
  gold-kintsugi cracks glowing under the strain; leaves and marble collide, dust and gold light bursting between them.
- **The Clash (close):** their palms meet; gold leaves and marble shards burst from the point of contact; kintsugi
  cracks race up Marmo's arm; Federica's gold veins flare.
- **Marmo Shatter:** the marble body cracks along its gold veins and bursts into a storm of marble shards and gold dust,
  then draws back inward and reforms.
(No dialogue in the duel; scored, wordless. It reads as two halves of one self fighting — gold warmth vs cold stone.)

## ACT V — DOUBLE DEATH (2:50–3:55) · Destrucción drop → aria peak → sudden silence
**Events.** Federica releases Marmo's hand and walks to the mirror; they stand side by side, facing the same way. In
the glass, OLD HUSAM still waits. Federica closes her eyes, opens them — a permission given like a breath. Marmo steps
through the glass into the mirror world and walks to Old Husam with the signature stride. He turns, recognizes her,
does not resist.
> **MARMO (TR, low — first time she speaks Turkish):** *"Sen artık taşıyamıyorsun. Bırak."* — (You can't carry this
> anymore. Let it go.)

**The kill / Petrify.** Marmo reaches out — not to the throat, not to the heart, but to his **FOREHEAD**. Her hand
rests there a moment. **Old Husam's face is visible this one time** — and he shows relief, a burden lifting; his flesh
turns to pale marble from the point of contact. He sinks to his knees, then falls.

On this side of the glass, Federica breaks:
> **FEDERICA (ES, cracked, loud):** *"¡Espera! ¡No!"* — (Wait! No!)

A single gold drop falls on her cheek — it does not flow, it stops. The mezzo-soprano aria peaks... then **TOTAL
SILENCE.**

**→ ENDING (director revision): HARD CUT TO BLACK on her scream.** What she lives through in that room afterward — the
marble softening back into the real Federica, Old Husam turning to dust, the renewal — is NOT shown on screen. It
happens off-screen, implied. Scream → black. That closes Act V. *(The screenplay's on-screen SK20–21 revelation and
"Eravamo entrambe" are cut from the picture.)*

## ACT VI — ŞİLE (3:55–4:45) · coda · song "Un Segundo Después"
**Events.** Weeks later. An overcast Black-Sea beach at Şile, fragile grey daylight, mid waves breaking. Federica sits
alone on the wet sand, back to camera — dark-blond hair down, black triangle bikini with snakeskin straps, her large
geisha back tattoo visible. A small camp table holds two mojitos. She is silent for a while.

Then a renewed **NEW HUSAM** sits down beside her — upright, open, face never shown; only two backs and the sea. She
hands him a mojito; their hands touch, unhurried — two old friends, nothing romantic. They look at the water.
> **HUSAM (EN + ES, quiet):** *"You taught me that — sorrow tiene su compás."* — (…sorrow has its rhythm.)
> **FEDERICA (IT, to herself, a broken small smile):** *[final-bridge line from "Un Segundo Después" — TBD from
> director; placeholder]*

The camera pulls back to a wide two-shot. They raise their plastic cups, clink softly, sip, and laugh. Warm, alive,
platonic. Fade toward black.

## AFTER CREDITS (4:45–4:55) · silent — THE SURPRISE
**Events.** Black. The surface of the empty Venetian mirror appears — no one, just glass. A single hairline crack runs
across it. From the crack, one gold drop seeps and descends slowly — **it does not flow, it stops** (rhyming exactly
with the gold drop on Federica's cheek in Act V). The gold — the power, Marmo — is not fully gone; a seed remains.
Then: **CUT TO BLACK. SON.**
